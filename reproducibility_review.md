## First copy-paste corrections

In chapters/03_methodology.tex, replace the paragraph beginning “The reproducibility framework consists of seven complementary stages.” with:

```latex
The reproducibility framework comprises complementary checks of manifest completeness, metadata consistency, attribution-map quality, numerical metrics, and analytical outputs. These checks establish different levels of evidence. Recalculating EDI from recorded Pearson correlation and SSIM verifies formula consistency; reproducing a table from a frozen manifest verifies its aggregation; recomputing those metrics from saved attribution arrays verifies the numerical comparison for the inspected pair and protocol. None of these checks alone establishes that the maps were generated by the intended models or that the recorded baseline belongs to the intended evaluation configuration. Baseline configuration, model identity, and attribution-generation settings therefore require separate provenance evidence. The completed internal map-pair reproduction covered 7,680 Grad-CAM and Grad-CAM++ comparisons. Additional audits of Eigen-CAM, Score-CAM, Integrated Gradients, and SHAP are assessed separately, and their unresolved lineage is not resolved by successful formula or aggregation checks.
```

In the same file, replace the paragraph beginning “Rather than relying on directory traversal or manual file matching” with:

```latex
The manifest records comparison keys and artifact references to support linkage between reported measurements and experimental outputs. These references are evaluated alongside source notebooks, saved numerical arrays, model records, and audit results. A populated identifier or file path is a recorded provenance claim rather than independent proof of model identity, correct baseline configuration, or historical attribution-generation settings. Downstream aggregation can be reproduced from a frozen manifest while these additional provenance questions remain unresolved.
```

These replacements distinguish the intended study design from the evidence actually established. They do not change numerical results or authorize canonical integration. Revisit the final sentence of the first replacement after the running audit is reviewed; a numerical match should not automatically remove the lineage qualification.
